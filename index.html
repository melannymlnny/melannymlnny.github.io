Side Project

Pempek Ceriyo — AI-assisted order workflow

Oct 2026 – present

Home-based frozen pempek business run with my mother · Tangerang, Indonesia

Pempek is a savoury fish cake from Palembang, South Sumatra, made from fish and tapioca and served with cuko, a sweet and sour vinegar sauce. We sell it frozen, ready to fry at home.

My mother cooks; I handle admin and promotion. Orders come in through pre-order batches on WhatsApp, so I set up a small system to keep the numbers reliable.

  • Wrote the operating procedure in Excel: a 34-step batch process, 15 WhatsApp reply templates and a 21-point checklist per batch.
  • Built a formula-driven order tracker (SUMIFS / COUNTIFS, dropdown validation) that produces a per-batch summary for production, unpaid orders and pickups.
  • Set up an AI-assisted workflow with Claude that logs clear orders, puts unclear ones on a “to check” list, and drafts replies that are sent only after I approve them.

The habits are the same ones I use in month-end work: one source for prices, formula-driven totals, an exceptions list, and a review step before anything goes out.
